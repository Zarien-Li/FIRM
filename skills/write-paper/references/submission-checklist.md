# Submission checklist

## Scientific

- contribution identity is stable and consistent across title, abstract,
  introduction, method, results, and conclusion;
- all headline claims map to raw evidence;
- strongest relevant baseline and fair controls are present or explicitly bounded;
- scope debt and limitations are visible;
- no failed-method relabeling without a surviving positive analysis object;
- no cherry-picked seeds, slices, or metrics.

## Citations and attribution

- citation audit completed;
- every borrowed dataset, codebase, model, and benchmark is attributed;
- no fabricated or placeholder references;
- related work represents close papers accurately.

## Artifact

- clean compile from the documented command;
- venue template and anonymity rules preserved;
- page, appendix, and supplementary limits checked against current official rules;
- figures legible at final size;
- PDF metadata, acknowledgements, and author information match submission mode;
- source archive contains every required file and excludes secrets or private data.

## Final human responsibility

A human author should inspect the final PDF, verify authorship and disclosure,
review all claims and citations, and make the final submission decision.
