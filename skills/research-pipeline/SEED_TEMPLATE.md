# Research Program Card

Use this for a fresh project. It opens a serious field of inquiry; it does not predict the final problem or method.

```markdown
# RESEARCH PROGRAM CARD

## Field And Value
- Broad field:
- Target venue and initial contribution ambition (provisional unless explicitly locked):
- What scientific or practical outcome would matter:
- Who would change a scientific, engineering, or operational decision if this work succeeded:
- Mainstream systems, populations, or workflows where that value lives:
- Why this is worth sustained work:

## Empirical Surface
- Accepted benchmark or real-workflow families:
- Representative SOTA and strong baseline families:
- Per-example behavior or traces worth preserving:
- Value metrics and important side effects:
- Natural-support evidence that an eventual problem should reconnect to:

## Research Lens
- Non-binding pressure or tension to inspect:
- Where this lead came from, if applicable: theory, system incident, user need, domain observation, or literature:
- Why it is plausible and important:
- Neighboring explanations that should remain open:

## Constraints
- Compute, model, data, and time constraints:
- Observability scope: what these resources can reveal reliably:
- Non-inferences: what absence under this scale/data/model cannot establish:
- Plausible escalation or surrogate if the value-bearing phenomenon is scale-dependent:
- User requirements:
- Contribution or deliverable explicitly locked by the user, if any:
- Contributions explicitly out of scope:

## Research Ownership
- The agent is the researcher responsible for discovering the problem, maintaining the thesis, proposing the method, and advancing the work within these constraints.
- A failed lead bet changes the thesis; it does not end the field.
- Related work becomes baseline material unless matched evidence shows exact closure.
```

Do not add a stop condition, pre-named mechanism, required method shape, or cheap-probe verdict to the initial card.

Do not make the project's continuation conditional on the first lens, predictor,
probe, dataset, or realization succeeding. A first empirical contact may reject its
hypothesis while leaving the authorized field and value fully alive. Conditions may
define what one experiment can establish; they may not define whether research is
allowed to continue.

A lead may come from theory, an operational failure, users, domain expertise, or a paper's limitation. Record that provenance without treating its framing as established fact. The empirical or formal work must be able to replace the lead with a different problem and explanation.

Constraints describe the current experimental surface; they are not scientific claims about the field. In particular, `<=8B`, one benchmark family, or one available model cannot justify concluding that a phenomenon or method opportunity does not exist outside that observability range. If the important behavior is not visible, record the limitation and decide whether to escalate, use a faithful surrogate, or choose a naturally visible problem.

After empirical work begins, keep the discovered problem, current thesis, contrary evidence, method lineage, and next question in the project's living research state. Do not wait for a ceremonial conversion into a second seed document.

The target venue is an ambition and evidence standard, not a promised outcome or a reason to distort the research question.

The seed defines the broader field, value, empirical surface, resources, and explicit user boundaries. Its initial contribution ambition is a lead, not the permanent identity of the current paper. Evidence may change the candidate paper identity without changing the seed. A living method contract, paper claim, or state summary must not silently overwrite this broader scope; only a contribution or deliverable explicitly locked by the user is binding.

The value fields are anchors, not a preselected failure or solution. They help later research distinguish a narrow diagnostic instrument from a problem whose solution would matter. Do not invent prevalence thresholds, required subgroups, or a mechanism in the seed. The eventual problem must earn its own importance while reconnecting to one of these real value surfaces.
