# Submission Maturation

Use this reference after the GPT PI can make an artifact-grounded argument that the
current object deserves focused paper work, or when a draft reveals a scientific gap.

## Form The Author Argument

Before outlining the final paper, write one page in ordinary prose answering:

- who the reader is and what they currently believe;
- which concrete fact changes that belief;
- why the change matters to research or practice;
- what simple principle or object the paper contributes;
- what the reader should think or do differently afterward.

Do not generate this page from a claims table, non-claim list, or experiment inventory.
An early provisional problem story may help research, but rewrite it once the evidence
and contribution stabilize.

## Learn Writing By Role, Not Averaging

Choose excellent full papers from respected venues and assign each a distinct role:
one may teach introduction movement, another results exposition, another discussion or
method presentation. A dedicated author subagent should read the complete role paper,
the project's evidence, and the author argument, then make a section draft with real
narrative choices. Do not average several papers into a generic template.

Use a human-reader editor rather than a hostile reviewer for prose. After ten minutes,
the reader should be able to retell the problem, surprising result, mechanism or method,
and consequence. Improve field-natural language, concrete nouns and verbs, paragraph
movement, specificity, authorial voice, and memorability. Do not optimize for defensive
caveat density.

## Use The Draft As An Integration Test

The first coherent manuscript exposes missing links between motivation, method,
evidence, and consequence. Classify a draft problem by what can resolve it: clearer
authorship, reanalysis of valid evidence, a missing decisive comparison or attribution,
a method defect or utility gap, or a contribution identity that is not independently
valuable.

Return scientific gaps to the research program. Do not hide them with narrower prose.
Conversely, do not open new experiments merely because an adversarial reviewer could
ask for them; ask whether the result can change the bounded paper.

## Verify After Writing

Build a claims-evidence map after a substantive draft exists. Verify every headline
number, comparison, qualifier, population, cost, and citation against raw artifacts and
primary sources. This table is a checking instrument, not the source of prose.

Keep caveats in the main argument only when they change interpretation. Put other honest
boundaries in limitations. Do not repeatedly state what the paper does not claim.

Compile and visually inspect the complete manuscript, figures, tables, references, and
supplement. Ensure artifacts, commands, and reported numbers agree. A final independent
review may target one concrete factual risk, but submission maturation is not an endless
cycle of objections.

## Completion Means A Human Can Sign

The GPT PI continues until the bounded paper has a coherent contribution, decisive
claim-dependent comparison, trustworthy evidence, understandable human writing,
venue-compliant package, and no unresolved issue that would materially change the
central claim. The user retains final factual, author, legal, and upload approval.

A method failure can motivate a new research question, but it cannot by itself supply
an analysis contribution. Remove the failed-method history mentally: if no important
phenomenon, consequence, audience, and evidence remain, there is no independent
non-method paper yet.
