# Writing A Research Program Card

A good entry card gives the researcher a consequential field, credible empirical contact, and enough freedom to discover the real problem.

## Write

- the field and desired change in the world;
- accepted benchmarks or real workflows;
- SOTA and strong baseline families worth reproducing;
- value metrics and side effects;
- a non-binding lens for reading failures;
- honest compute, data, venue, and contribution constraints.

## Do Not Write

- a mechanism disguised as a problem;
- a paper's limitation as though it were independently established;
- a method operator before local evidence exists;
- a premise probe as the project's stopping rule;
- a benchmark or dataset paper when the intended contribution is a method;
- a strawman baseline or an obscure slice chosen to manufacture daylight.

## Seven Research Habits

1. Enter through field, value, benchmark, and SOTA rather than a favored mechanism.
2. Treat the first hypothesis as orientation, not identity.
3. Let theory, incidents, users, domain observations, or papers suggest leads, but independently earn the final problem and explanation from the evidence appropriate to the contribution.
4. Check that solving the failure changes something users or scientists value.
5. Match the empirical scale to the phenomenon; absence at a toy scale limits the test, not necessarily the field.
6. Treat active related work as a positive importance prior and stronger baseline material, while checking that the activity reflects real value rather than leaderboard fashion.
7. When a simple baseline wins, explain its hidden variable and natural failure boundary before changing fields.

## Quality Test

The card is ready when another researcher could enter the field, reproduce meaningful systems, and discover a different valid problem than the author initially imagined.

It is too narrow when one null result would make the entire card meaningless. It is too broad when it does not identify credible empirical contact or a value-bearing outcome.

Venue or spotlight ambition sets the bar; it does not guarantee acceptance or authorize claim inflation.
