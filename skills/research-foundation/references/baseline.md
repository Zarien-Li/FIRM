# Baseline And Empirical Contact

Build a trustworthy contact point with the field, then read what serious systems do.
Baseline evidence establishes reality and ownership; it does not choose the paper
identity or close the program.

## Name Comparators Precisely

- A **published-method baseline** is a named method or system from the relevant
  literature with its paper, venue/year, operational semantics, implementation, and
  evaluation protocol identified.
- A **backbone or substrate control** is an unmodified foundation model, retriever,
  simulator, or execution system used to establish base competence and residual
  headroom.
- A **matched control or ablation** isolates the project's treatment, component, or
  training change for attribution.

When asked to run “the baseline,” reproduce methods proposed in recent field papers,
not merely raw Qwen, Llama, CLIP, diffusion, or analogous checkpoints. Retain an older
method when it remains a recognized incumbent. Do not invent weak project-specific
heuristics to fill the table; a simple method belongs when it is a recognized published
or field-standard comparator for the accepted protocol.

Before reporting field contact or optimizing a proposed method, reproduce at least one
strong recent published method and identify the strongest functional and nearest
mechanistic rivals, or record the concrete blocker and keep method claims provisional.

## Give Baselines Different Scientific Jobs

The comparison set grows with the decision, not with a fixed paper count:

- **field contact:** one competent recent incumbent on the accepted surface, raw
  behavior, and the closest operational and mechanistic neighbors;
- **candidate judgment:** the realized method against the strongest functional rival,
  nearest mechanistic rival, and matched attribution control that could erase its value
  or novelty;
- **submission claim:** the bounded published methods, accepted surfaces, model regimes,
  reliability, and efficiency evidence owed by the actual claim.

Exploratory construction may proceed while an expensive comparator is being repaired,
but the PI must keep ownership and maturity provisional. A user-commissioned baseline-
first matrix may precede method formation: complete its meaningful non-method comparisons
and use their behavior to inform invention. Do not invoke candidate maturity as a reason
to ignore that commission. Otherwise expand comparisons for the live scientific decision,
not merely to manufacture progress or fill a submission table.

## Choose Existing Code Before Writing A Harness

Start from the accepted task, intended main-table conditions, and strongest published
comparisons. Look at the official repositories and maintained reproductions before
building replacements. Choose the code base by the behavior it can reproduce: a
verifiable strong method, real benchmark inputs and raw outputs, scorer fidelity,
target-backbone fit, and the size of any required semantic changes. README coverage,
recency, and stars are not reproduction evidence. Pin the version and record what is
reused, adapted, or unsupported in the existing comparison record.

Reuse verified loaders, evaluators, and orchestration where their semantics agree.
Keep the authors' method-specific training or inference logic when sharing a trainer
would alter it. A common table does not require a common implementation. If no single
repository fits, combine faithful upstream paths at the output/evaluation boundary;
write only the missing pieces. When several plausible bases differ materially and the
choice affects a costly campaign, a fresh bounded reader can independently compare them
from the task, strong rivals, and protocol, without the PI's preferred repository.

A faithful adaptation can enter the main table when its semantics and conditions justify
the comparison. A proxy, incomplete hook, or substitute must not inherit the published
method's name or evidential standing. Label departures at the result.

Design the most consequential attribution comparison alongside the first informative
realization. Ask what ordinary training, added data, supervision, compute, or inference
procedure could explain its gain without the proposed principle. Resolve that contrast
before interpreting the principle as effective or scaling on that interpretation.
This is not a demand for the entire submission table: choose the comparison that
actually separates the live explanations. A cheaper faithful configuration can be useful;
formal comparisons still obey the complete-data protocol below.

## Build A Comparable Field Matrix

Synthesize the full-paper method map from `research-lit.md` into chosen benchmarks,
backbone regimes, published methods, and protocols. Explain why they share the same
canonical task and operational comparison. Choose the main surfaces from recurring
accepted protocols and the project's intended value, not whichever dataset is easiest.
Requested method and benchmark counts guide coverage; they cannot make an incompatible
comparison valid.

Distinguish native reproduction from a common-backbone comparison. The former checks
the original method in its documented regime; the latter applies methods to matched
backbones, data, supervision, training and inference budgets, and evaluators where their
semantics permit. When the user requests modern checkpoints such as Qwen3, honor that
choice in the common-backbone matrix without pretending it was the paper's original
configuration. Verify each adaptation's essential computation and competence. A method
bound to a tokenizer, architecture, modality, or released checkpoint may not transfer;
explain the incompatibility rather than substituting an incomplete operator.

Multiple backbone families belong in the main comparison when commissioned or needed
by the claim, not only in a separate cross-family appendix. Do not claim cross-family
generality by evaluating the proposed method on several families while its decisive
rivals appear on only one. Nor must every method run on every backbone and benchmark:
select semantically meaningful cells and explain exclusions. Published scores may be
context, but must not masquerade as locally reproduced cells under changed conditions.

Preview the actual intended tables before substantial execution. Keep the method rows,
benchmark/backbone columns, metric direction, protocol, evidence role, and costs legible.
Use `-` for unrun numbers, including the proposed method; state separately whether a
cell is planned, incompatible, blocked, or failed so a dash cannot hide an obligation.
These are comparison plans, not a fabricated manuscript or evidence of paper maturity.
Keep native and adapted protocols visibly distinct. Populate results from validated
artifacts through the existing table entrypoint, not manual transcription.

Use `COMPARISON_CONTRACT.json` as the versioned source of evaluation meaning, and
validated run artifacts as the source of numbers. Use the existing baseline record
or `BASELINE_REPRO.md` for source-backed behavioral and reproduction findings; do not
maintain a conflicting protocol copy there. Connect table cells to
stable campaign/run identities and record source-backed selection, adaptations,
incompatibilities, and unresolved details there; do not create another independent
live project state. Plan forecast and execution through `experiment-plan` and
`experiment-operations`. Completion includes scoring, coverage, and raw behavioral
reading, not only a launch or a set of checkpoints.

## Preserve The Main Comparison While Improving The Method

Recover the intended main-table comparison from explicit user instructions, the accepted
benchmark protocol, and the baseline artifacts. A frozen CSV or score vector alone is
not an evaluation contract. Keep enough source-linked meaning in the comparison
contract to know what each cell measures: input information/context and model regime;
data revision, preprocessing, split and eligible population; task/target/horizon;
evaluator, metric units, aggregation and failure handling; checkpoint/variant selection
and any agreed resource or tuning conditions. Record what remains unknown instead of
assuming that matching dataset names and output lengths makes scores comparable.

Develop the proposed method for that comparison, not under an easier or richer condition
whose relationship to the main table will be considered only at writing time. The PI
owns method, architecture, loss, optimization and implementation choices within the
comparison's permitted freedoms. Fairness need not mean identical learning rates,
optimizers or epoch counts; establish competent method-specific training and disclose
selection effort. Conversely, calling context, normalization, data cleaning or model
selection a "protocol variable" does not establish that a change is permitted or that
old comparator scores remain usable.

An accepted protocol may allow method-specific context lengths or native recipes;
establish that freedom from its actual sources rather than imposing universal equality.
Likewise, model-internal normalization can be part of the method when available inputs
and the scored target/units remain unchanged. Distinguish such design choices from
changing the evaluated information, target scaling or metric. Judge the actual effect,
not the configuration field's name.

Native reproduction and off-condition diagnosis can be useful without replacing the
main comparison. Before building on such a result, explain what it can teach and how
the resulting construction will answer the intended comparison. Do not count its wins,
margin or extra seeds as progress already achieved against the main-table methods.
If the method requires different input information, preprocessing, target, selection or
scoring semantics, report that difference and its comparison consequence. Neither
"native recipe" nor faithful implementation licenses silently changing our column
while retaining the other columns' old scores.

A scientifically justified new regime can support a separately identified matched
comparison. Preserve the original commissioned obligation and reuse earlier artifacts
only where their semantics still match; recompute only genuinely affected evidence.
Do not rerun every baseline merely because software or hardware differs. Trace whether
the difference changes the measured behavior, numerical result, or cost claim. Exact
artifact identifiers aid reconstruction; neither matching hashes nor different hashes
alone decide scientific comparability.

These are PI judgments, not a permission gate. Run useful diagnostic work and ordinary
authorized optimization autonomously. Explain a proposed revision of the comparison
and its evidence consequences in the existing record; a user-locked condition remains
binding unless the user changes it. Do not treat revising an agent-authored plan as a
new human approval requirement, nor silently replace a commissioned main comparison
with the diagnostic one. Missing protocol provenance leaves the affected comparison
unresolved, not the whole research program blocked or all completed evidence invalid.
Use the comparison template and immutable run bindings in
[research-tree.md](../../research-pipeline/references/research-tree.md). Record actual
departures beside the result as expected condition, observed condition, and consequence.
Changing a backbone, retrieval source, or training class can be a method treatment or a
different scientific comparison; explain which, rather than imposing a field-name stop
rule. While unresolved, retain the run's diagnostic value but do not merge its wins into
the commissioned table. Preserve prior contracts rather than editing their history.

## Use Complete Official Data Protocols

For this portfolio, formal baseline, attribution, and method comparisons use the full
specified dataset splits, not a convenient sampled subset. Honor each official protocol's
train/development/test separation, prescribed folds, temporal boundaries, and eligible
population. Full data does not mean pooling splits or altering the task. A method's
intrinsic selection rule must remain faithful and disclosed; it is different from
sampling the experiment away to reduce cost.

A small real-path canary may verify execution, engagement, memory, and throughput. Label
it as such and do not place its score in a completed comparison cell or use it to replace
full-split evidence. If the full protocol exceeds authorized resources, forecast and
escalate the actual gap rather than silently sampling. This data requirement does not
mandate every conceivable dataset, seed, or matrix cross-product.

In a baseline-first program, choose the data used for behavioral reading and invention
explicitly. Use training/development cases to select explanations, prompts, architecture,
or hyperparameters; keep confirmatory test cases and per-example outputs separate from
that work. Completing a baseline's full official evaluation does not authorize using its
test failures to tune the proposed method. Published or recorded aggregate test scores
can describe the field, but do not turn test-guided construction into independent
confirmation. Preserve any prior exposure and its consequence for the claim.

## Establish An Accepted And Deployable Surface

Name one `benchmark_anchor`: a standard task, deployed workflow, or natural population
on which the field recognizes progress. Label other evidence as claim-bearing, training,
or diagnostic. Synthetic cases, generated data, or project-defined slices can expose a
mechanism but do not replace the anchor without independent field value and deliberate
program re-grounding.

Confirm that representative examples actually instantiate the canonical object and the
primary outcome can register its improvement. A truncated task, insensitive aggregate
metric, or hand-selected clean region may be useful diagnostically but cannot carry the
problem or paper identity.

Also list what information each system uses at the moment a deployed decision must be
made. Oracle regions, gold boundaries, hidden labels, future context, privileged
annotations, and clean subsets can locate headroom; they do not establish a deployable
opening. Before method formation, realize the principle from available inputs or retain
it as an unresolved diagnostic opportunity.

## Verify Substrate And Regime Competence

Confirm that the underlying model and execution path competently perform the standard
task without the proposed primitive. If not, mark the evidence `substrate-confounded`
and repair the substrate before attributing a package difference to the method.

Record the intended and actual model scale, context length, training regime, modality,
distribution, and deployment boundary. Reproduce the incumbent's motivating positive
behavior in the intended regime before building around its residual failure. A cheaper
proxy is claim-bearing only when evidence shows that it preserves the phenomenon, rival
ordering, and intervention opportunity. A proxy-specific failure does not redefine the
project without its own accepted value and rivals.

## Reproduce Semantics And Resolve Rivals

Before scaling a consequential path, compare actual local behavior with an expectation
derived independently of the local port and its tests. Use the paper, appendix, pinned
upstream execution, or a known example under the benchmark's scoring rules. Verify a
real strong-method path, including the defining computation and the real evaluator on
known outputs, then reuse that evidence wherever the relevant path is unchanged. For
example, verify the actual checkpoint used to initialize a generation when the method
requires reset-to-base; finishing both generations does not verify that transition.
A canary proves only the behaviors it checked, not full-run competence or performance.

When an ambiguous detail would affect many cells, use a fresh subagent if useful.
Give it the sources and target protocol, not the local implementation or verdict.
Ask for the expected behavior, source location, and observable evidence that would
distinguish it from a plausible wrong port. For scoring, share the rules and raw answer
without the local score. The PI reconciles expectations with the actual execution,
then repairs only the affected path or keeps its evidence unresolved. Direct source
verification suffices when the answer is clear; this is not routine double review.
Use [collaboration.md](../../research-pipeline/references/collaboration.md) for the
bounded handoff and [experiment-integrity.md](experiment-integrity.md) for evidence
requirements. Propagate verified invalidation through
[evidence-lineage.md](../../research-pipeline/references/shared/evidence-lineage.md).

Before novelty lock, identify the two or three closest approaches by operational effect
and mechanism. Ask whether they absorb the proposed principle or erase its practical
value. Repeat when the method's actual job or deployment contract changes. If a local
reproduction remains materially below the paper under the same protocol, reconcile the
checkpoint, data, evaluator, optimization, and reporting semantics or keep the anchor
unresolved; do not freeze the weaker number as the incumbent.

During prolonged reproduction repair, reassess whether the current implementation is
still the best route to trustworthy field contact. Consider a compatible pinned
environment, another faithful implementation, or a relevant runnable incumbent, while
retaining unresolved comparison obligations. Document semantic changes to training
loci or evaluators rather than calling a mechanically runnable substitute faithful.

Revisit that choice when actual repair cost diverges from the forecast or reproduction
starts displacing the live construction question. Explain what this comparison can
still change and whether another faithful route provides the needed evidence. Keep a
necessary decisive rival even when expensive; defer comparators that add coverage but
cannot affect the current decision. Neither a fixed paper quota nor a universal
GPU-hour cutoff decides this tradeoff.
If a decisive published baseline remains too expensive, retain that comparison debt.
Seek a faithful implementation, compatible reusable artifacts, or a genuinely cheaper
equivalent execution path. If none fits, the PI requests the actual resource gap or
proposes an honest claim/comparison revision without silently replacing the commission.
A homemade solver, incomplete port, or cheaper proxy cannot inherit the published
method's name or main-table standing. Development can continue, but cannot erase that
missing decisive rival or treat its unmeasured score as beaten.

## Read Behavior, Not Only Scores

Read aligned outputs from competent methods on natural development cases. Use
comparable successes to see what the incumbent already does well, and failures and
rival disagreements to locate what might need to change. Keep contrary cases that
distinguish different design responses. Separate measurement or execution failures
from model behavior. Give the PI the observations and artifact locations that
matter, not just another metric or a large case dump. Use an existing development
split or independently admissible evidence for design; do not turn the held-out
claim split into a development set. A rare slice can clarify a mechanism without
becoming the research destination.

Add another model, dataset, regime, or seed only to resolve a named uncertainty about
shared behavior, implementation artifacts, recognized comparators, transfer, scale,
utility, or claim scope. A competent bad seed triggers diagnosis before expansion; it
does not close the method family or broad program.

## Record Provenance And Health

Record source, version or commit, checkpoint, environment, local changes, and why a
stronger implementation was unavailable. State any concrete defect or unresolved
semantic difference and the claims it prevents. Evidence health describes the
comparison; it does not decide the method, paper, or program.

`method-development` owns explaining that behavior and deciding what to build.
