# Independent paper-review rubric

Evaluate the current artifact without prior-round context.

## Scientific core

- importance and specificity of the problem;
- clarity of the positive object and contribution identity;
- necessity and novelty of the method primitive or thesis;
- fairness of baselines and controls;
- strength of evidence and uncertainty;
- claim scope, limitations, and alternative explanations.

## Exposition

- abstract fidelity;
- introduction logic and contribution bullets;
- method reproducibility;
- results organization and figure/table interpretation;
- related-work accuracy;
- notation, terminology, and cross-section consistency;
- readability of figures and captions;
- conclusion and limitations.

For every blocking or major issue, give the exact location, why it matters, the
smallest defensible repair, and whether the repair needs new evidence or only better
writing.
