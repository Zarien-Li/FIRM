# Independent Model Collaboration

The main researcher owns the evolving thesis, method, experiment choice, and paper decision. Other models provide independent scientific viewpoints; they are not fixed stations in a pipeline and do not hold authority to approve or terminate research.

## Roles Follow The Question

Choose a model because an independent perspective would improve a live decision:

- reinterpret contradictory evidence;
- expose a hidden variable behind a simple-baseline win;
- invent a method at a different design altitude;
- attack a method's necessity, naturality, or utility tradeoff;
- audit whether an existing bounded contribution is ready to consolidate.

Do not permanently assign one model to scouting, one to judging, and one to architecture. The lead researcher must perform serious interpretation and invention before requesting review. An external model may interpret, invent, attack, or consolidate depending on the question.

Routine implementation, monitoring, evidence bookkeeping, and decisions already determined by clear local evidence do not need ceremonial multi-model review.

## Independence

Use a fresh thread when independence matters. Give the smallest sufficient evidence packet:

- current thesis and confidence;
- representative successes, failures, and inconvenient cases;
- exact result conditions and registered forecast;
- strongest contrary evidence;
- method lineage;
- closest incumbent and strongest simple alternative;
- the decision that remains genuinely ambiguous.

Do not lead with a desired verdict, a stopping recommendation, or another reviewer's conclusion. Raw artifacts and exact tables are preferable to persuasive summaries.

Continue the same thread only when debating the same unresolved scientific question with corrected assumptions or new evidence. Do not open repeated fresh threads to shop for agreement.

## Productive Review Shape

For consequential ambiguity, ask the independent model to:

1. **Interpret:** explain the full positive, negative, and inconvenient pattern and locate the first unsupported part of the method-to-claim argument.
2. **Invent:** propose a higher-level method or discriminating experiment if stopping were unavailable.
3. **Attack:** give the strongest artifact, simple composition, utility cost, or reviewer objection.
4. **Consolidate:** identify what bounded contribution already survives and whether research or paper completion has higher expected value.

Require constructive continuation before adversarial criticism. A reviewer should help improve and finish good work, not merely issue a verdict.

## Lead-Researcher Synthesis

The main researcher decides what to adopt. Record:

- insight adopted;
- suggestion rejected and evidence;
- thesis or method change;
- effect on the current paper claim;
- next action and why;
- evidence that would change the decision.

Agreement is not empirical closure. Disagreement is not a command to run more experiments. Evaluate both against local evidence and the value of the current paper.

## Tool Failure

An unavailable reviewer is an infrastructure issue. Preserve the evidence packet, perform an explicit self-adversarial pass as the lead researcher, and continue work that does not depend on independent judgment. Do not pause research, guess model names, or convert tool failure into approval or rejection.

## Structured Handoffs

Prefer durable Markdown or JSON containing exact paths, configurations, tables, raw-case identifiers, and scope. Do not rely on session memory or vague statements about what another model meant. Structure is for provenance and continuity, not a mandatory form or permission gate.
