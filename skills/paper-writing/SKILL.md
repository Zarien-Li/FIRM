# Paper Writing

Own the manuscript from an early problem story through a submission-ready package.
Writing integrates the science for a human reader; it neither substitutes for missing
evidence nor turns review into an objection checklist.

## Route The Work

- `plan`, `write`, `full`, or `research-draft`: read
  [paper-writing.md](references/paper-writing.md).
- `improvement`: after the contribution and evidence are stable, read
  [auto-paper-improvement-loop.md](references/auto-paper-improvement-loop.md).
- `figures`, `compile`, `claim-audit`, or `submission`: read the matching reference
  linked from `paper-writing.md`.
- `resubmit`: for a bounded transfer of a frozen paper to another venue, read
  [resubmit-pipeline.md](references/resubmit-pipeline.md).

Load only the reference needed for the current work. These modes share one manuscript
owner, one contribution identity, and one evidence lineage.
