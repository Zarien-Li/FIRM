# Method Primitive Synthesis

Build the method from the explanation. The method should make the desired behavior more natural by design, not merely detect or compensate for the symptom after it appears.

This skill owns method-space exploration as well as primitive specification. Do not generate ideas in one workflow, score them in another, and synthesize a method in a third. Widen only enough to expose genuinely different causal bets, choose a provisional scaffold, and let informative construction experiments form the method. Commitment means taking responsibility for evolving the strongest current bet, not pretending its first specification is final.

## Start From The Thesis

State:

- the original research program and the current paper's relationship to it;
- the natural failure class;
- the discovery slice, if narrower than the intended problem;
- the intended method population and evidence that it has meaningful natural support;
- the value consequence;
- the current causal explanation;
- the strongest contrary evidence;
- the design assumption implicated by the explanation;
- lessons inherited from earlier method attempts.

Also establish why a method opportunity exists. Look for value headroom on the accepted task, information available at the proposed intervention point, a coherent enough failure class to admit a shared remedy, meaningful natural support, and a natural boundary of the strongest incumbent. Oracle or diagnostic success can motivate this judgment, but it does not by itself show that a deployed learner can identify or actuate the correction. A primitive that wins only inside the slice that selected it has not yet established broad design leverage.

Name the shared-incumbent defect rather than only the selected cell. If the current method requires increasingly specific qualifiers, carry that scope debt explicitly and state how the primitive could repay it: return to a standard task or system, alter ordinary behavior across serious incumbents, transfer as one conditional principle, or establish an independently consequential rare case. The discovery slice may train the eye and shape the mechanism; it should not become the method's population by inertia.

If the explanation is only “the model is bad at X,” the method locus is not ready.

## Do Not Shop For A Substrate

Choose the problem before optimizing for the candidate method. Do not select a dataset, operator, threshold band, architecture family, or synthetic regime because it gives the primitive headroom, cleaner identification, or a positive result. Such settings may diagnose a mechanism, but they cannot carry the method claim unless their natural importance was established independently.

Before committing, name the shared mainstream assumption changed, the value-bearing population beyond the discovery cell, and the standard-task or system test through which the intervention returns to the original seed. If no reintegration path exists, return to broad empirical contact rather than adding qualifiers or searching for a friendlier substrate.

## Widen At The Level Of Cause

Generate a small number of candidates that differ in explanatory account or design altitude. Look across representation and state, update and read rules, routing and memory, objective factorization and credit assignment, training interaction and curriculum, data construction, and system execution semantics. Question the current abstraction when every candidate accepts the same suspect primitive.

Do not count prompt edits, threshold variants, weight sweeps, probe heads, local policies, verifier wrappers, or renamed versions of one operator as a diverse method space. Such devices may be strong baselines or useful components; they become the contribution only when evidence shows that they express the load-bearing design principle rather than surround unchanged machinery.

For each serious candidate, ask:

- which positive, negative, and inconvenient observations it explains;
- which load-bearing assumption it changes;
- what prediction distinguishes it from competing candidates;
- how it differs from the strongest incumbent and simple composition under matched conditions;
- what value, general-capability, coverage, latency, or cost tradeoff it creates;
- what the smallest real implementation would teach even if it loses.

Also state its positive research form:

```text
Replacement principle:
One-sentence method:
Default assumption replaced:
Research leverage:
Adoption path:
Expansion axes: architecture | supervision/data | scale | task/model | mechanism
```

These fields are not marketing copy. If they require the method name, benchmark, and
several private qualifiers to sound important, the candidate has not yet escaped the
discovery cell.

Do not brainstorm from literature terminology alone. A candidate needs an empirical or formal reason to exist.

## Compare, Steelman, And Choose

Judge candidates by problem value at the current specificity, natural support, explanatory compression, causal handle, leverage across mainstream systems or ordinary behavior, irreducibility, feasible learning, attainable headroom, and what they inherit from prior attempts. These are lenses, not a scoring formula. Prefer a primitive that changes a shared load-bearing assumption over a highly customized fix for the cleanest cell. Do not choose a harder primitive merely because it sounds more architectural or novel; a method must have a credible route to learning and task value before its distinctiveness matters.

Before major implementation, imagine the strongest credible success rather than the minimum passing result. State the paper-level object that perfect execution would create and the reintegration experiment that would demonstrate value on an accepted surface. If success would still leave only a bespoke correction for the discovery cell, widen the causal account or return to empirical contact before investing.

Steelman before rejecting: use the correct regime, strongest plausible realization, and semantically matched baseline. Related work raises the bar and supplies compromises to improve on; a similar operator name does not close the method unless matched evidence shows it resolves the same natural failure without the claimed cost. Do not seek novelty by intersecting qualifiers until no paper occupies the cell. Ask what important compromise the active literature repeatedly accepts and whether the proposed primitive removes it.

Do not end with an unranked menu. Select the strongest method bet, synthesize a better one, or run one discriminating experiment when the causal accounts are genuinely unresolved. If no candidate is convincing because the problem class or value is artificial, return to empirical contact. If several same-level candidates fail for one demonstrated reason, raise the shared design assumption toward a broader value-bearing system defect rather than opening another local variant or descending into a more private internal variable.

## Form The Method Through Constructive Ablation

Treat every proposed component as a causal bet with a job, not as a decoration to be justified after the full system is built. Develop the method through comparisons that add, remove, replace, freeze, reroute, or factorize components and then inspect both target behavior and side effects. An ablation is method invention when its result changes what the next implementation contains or how its parts interact.

Do not restrict formation to leave-one-out tables. When components may compensate for, gate, or interfere with one another, use paired substitutions or compact factorial designs that expose interactions. Compare matched information, supervision, parameter capacity, training budget, and optimization where those differences could explain the outcome. Inspect learning dynamics and raw component behavior so a failed combination can reveal which element never performed its intended role.

Allow a component to disappear. If a simpler composition preserves the useful behavior, removing the redundant component is progress toward the method rather than a failed novelty claim. If a component works only with another, the interaction may be the positive design object. Keep a concise lineage of which construction changed which belief; do not generate a cemetery of named variants.

Development ablations may select the design. They must retain honest forecasts and results, but they need not be treated as independent confirmation. After the method stabilizes, rerun the decisive necessity and interaction claims on appropriate fresh evidence when those claims carry the paper.

Method stabilization is earned when the main components have identifiable jobs, the important interactions have been exercised, the intended computation is active, and remaining changes are mostly optimization or evidence completion. It is not earned merely because several variants have failed or one relation has become statistically stable.

Before each construction episode, name the paper asset it can create and its expected
delta over the current evidence set. A valid target may be an executable realization,
a decisive anchor comparison, a necessity result, a paired utility result, or a
principle-predicted transfer. Continue the same lineage after a competent negative only
when accepted-anchor evidence supports a still-untested load-bearing prediction. There
is no fixed episode limit, but a new name, private slice, or richer explanation is not
by itself a reason to spend another episode.

## Expand A Credible Primitive

Once a realization has credible positive end-to-end value, test whether its governing
principle deserves a larger research program. Seek connected contributions, not a bag
of experiments: a reusable training or execution primitive, a natural data-production
system, predicted cross-task or cross-model behavior, a scale law, or mechanism
evidence that changes how the primitive should be used.

Apply the 80% deletion test: remove most modules, names, and engineering details. If a
strong new principle and adoption path remain, strengthen that core. If nothing
remains except a benchmark-specific stack, do not spend a paper-sized budget making
the stack harder to criticize.

Expansion begins only after a credible realization. It must not rescue a failed method,
replace missing natural evidence with more datasets, or treat scale as causal proof.

## Evolve A Method From Its Failure

This skill also applies after a real method has been trained and missed its target. Preserve the honest verdict for that run, then use the failure mechanism to design the next realization.

Ask:

- which parts of the method demonstrably worked;
- where the method-to-value chain first broke;
- whether the learned component was dormant, mis-optimized, mis-routed, under-supported, or solving the wrong proxy;
- which load-bearing component can change the failed behavior without discarding the useful behavior;
- what unique prediction the revised method makes over both its predecessor and the simple baseline.

A revised method is scientifically legitimate when the redesign follows prospectively from observed failure structure. It is not legitimate when it merely changes thresholds or success definitions until a number passes.

Distinguish the verdict on a particular run from the maturity of the method realization and from the status of the underlying primitive. A first prototype may be sufficient to test activation or expose interference while being too weakly optimized, integrated, or scaled for a final incumbent comparison. Preserve its negative headline result, but do not promote prototype immaturity into a primitive-level conclusion.

Do not use immaturity to protect a weak idea indefinitely. A primitive-level negative becomes credible when its intended computation is active, the realization has received reasonable optimization and scale within the agreed budget, matched alternatives still explain or outperform it, and no useful residual points to a specific repair. At that point change the primitive or abstraction while preserving the problem and exact lessons learned.

When useful behavior appears together with a cost elsewhere, treat the pair as a design gradient. Locate which pathway creates the gain and which coupling creates the regression; then build the next version to preserve the former and alter the latter. A method lineage should become more competent and more explanatory, not merely accumulate nearby variants.

When outcomes vary by reader, model, dataset, scale, query, or system state, do not immediately declare the primitive non-general. Ask whether the current method incorrectly treats a load-bearing context variable as fixed. If that variable is available or learnable under deployment constraints, design a shared conditional principle: represent it, route on it, jointly learn it, or factor the method so each component acts where its assumption holds. Generality may be an adaptive rule that explains heterogeneous actions, not one unchanged action everywhere.

This redesign is legitimate only when the contextual variable predicts or explains the variation beyond a post-hoc dataset name and the adaptive mechanism can be tested against strong fixed and simple conditional alternatives. Otherwise return to the broader failure class rather than accumulating qualifiers.

Revisit accumulated restrictions before each substantial redesign. Keep genuine deployment constraints and evidence-backed invariants. Drop prohibitions inherited from an obsolete framing, convenience choices, or earlier attempts when they prevent the only plausible actuation path. If relaxing a user constraint would materially change the opportunity, surface that tradeoff rather than declaring the science exhausted inside an artificially empty design space.

## Find The Violated Invariant

Ask what the system should preserve, separate, represent, allocate, update, or optimize but currently does not. Examples include:

- evidence identity across transformations;
- causal or temporal state consistency;
- group-conditional risk without utility collapse;
- credit assigned to the component that created the outcome;
- persistent information not forced into destructive competition;
- uncertainty represented where a decision is made;
- execution semantics preserved across reuse or repair.

The invariant should explain both failures and successes. Avoid inventing one that merely restates the evaluation metric.

## Localize The Design Assumption

Identify the smallest load-bearing design location:

- representation or state object;
- attention, routing, retrieval, or memory update;
- training objective or factorization;
- optimizer or credit assignment;
- data curriculum or interaction protocol;
- system abstraction, storage, or execution contract.

Then ask whether changing that location is sufficient in principle, and what side effects it may create elsewhere.

## Invert Strong Simple Baselines

When a threshold, homophily rule, cache key, solver, verifier, calibration method, or other simple baseline wins, do not merely avoid it. Explain:

- why it works;
- what hidden coordinate it exposes;
- where it naturally fails;
- why the proposed primitive handles that boundary by construction;
- which simple composition remains a serious competitor.

This often raises the method from a local patch to a useful design principle.

If the winning baseline is an external solver, deterministic procedure, retrieval pipeline, or tool wrapper, do not conclude that a learned internal method is pointless. Extract the state, invariants, execution semantics, or information-selection principle that makes it work. The method opportunity may be to make that computation native, learned, differentiable, efficient, or robust under constraints where the external composition is unavailable or too costly.

## Test Causal Necessity

Before a large run, identify an economical experiment that distinguishes:

- the proposed causal primitive;
- a predictive diagnostic using the same signal;
- the strongest simple alternative;
- a version where the apparent win is bought by refusal, deletion, abstention, latency, or capability loss.

Interventions on hidden states, KV entries, embeddings, or internal features can create positional or distributional artifacts. Design controls that preserve unrelated computation and measure the actual task outcome.

The economical experiment is a learning instrument, not a universal precondition and not necessarily the final method evaluation. If it shows that the primitive activates and creates useful behavior, invest in a competent realization before asking it to satisfy the full paper claim. If it shows that the intended computation never occurred, repair execution or optimization before drawing scientific conclusions.

## Prefer Positive Design Objects

Describe what the paper creates: a state representation, training axis, update rule, execution semantic, memory primitive, or optimization principle. Do not define contribution mainly as “unlike prior work, we do not have flaw X.”

The method can be small. It should not be conceptually reducible to a collection of familiar controls without evidence that the new object is necessary.

## Second-PI Method Challenge

Before a major implementation commitment, invoke `/firm:second-pi` when the primitive's necessity, design altitude, or distinction from a simple composition remains genuinely uncertain. Supply the natural failure class, thesis, contrary evidence, proposed primitive, strongest simple alternative, and utility risks.

Ask the independent reviewer to perform three moves:

- **Interpret:** identify the design assumption that best explains the full evidence;
- **Invent:** propose the strongest higher-level primitive if the current one is merely a patch;
- **Attack:** construct the simplest composition or causal objection that could make both unnecessary.

If the review exposes a better locus, redesign before spending. If it only requests ceremonial checklist experiments, retain the stronger scientific plan and explain why. The lead researcher remains responsible for the method choice.
