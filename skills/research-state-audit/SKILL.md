# Research State Audit

Use this audit at consequential boundaries. Do not block ordinary reading, diagnosis, ideation, or small experiments because a ceremonial artifact is missing.
For contribution-identity changes and complete manuscript entry, also follow
[the research control protocol](../shared-references/research-control-protocol.md).

Do not schedule this audit periodically and do not invoke it merely because another skill finished. Routine thesis updates belong in `research-pipeline`; consequential completed results belong in `signal-analysis`. This audit is reserved for decisions whose integrity or irreversibility justifies an independent boundary check.

## Choose The Audit Boundary

Name the decision under review:

- launch a claim-defining or unusually expensive run;
- make a major method or scope commitment;
- write or submit a paper claim;
- decide whether another research branch is worth more than finishing a mature current contribution;
- consider a portfolio-level program pause;
- resume after a long interruption or contradictory state.

Apply only the checks relevant to that decision.

## Evidence Integrity

Verify:

- data, split, checkpoint, prompt/protocol, and candidate pool;
- evaluator, parser, metric definition, and direction;
- baseline semantic fidelity;
- information, supervision, labels, tools, and external-computation parity;
- strongest direct, deterministic, algorithmic, or simple alternative;
- leakage, circular labels, truncation, filtering, and fallback behavior;
- exact configs, commands, seeds, logs, checkpoints, and result paths;
- completed, running, interrupted, unread, and invalidated runs;
- whether post-repair conclusions were rerun or explicitly limited.

Any claim relying on uncertain evidence remains blocked until repaired. This does not block continued investigation.

## Research Understanding

Check whether the current state explains:

- field and value;
- central thesis and confidence;
- strongest supporting and contrary evidence;
- natural failure class and successes for contrast;
- unresolved contradictions;
- method lineage and what failures taught;
- strongest simple alternative;
- best next question and action.

These may live in one concise `PIPELINE_STATE.md` block or equivalent. Separate `QUESTION_LEDGER`, `ROOT_CAUSE_AUDIT`, `TASTE_REVIEW`, and `OWNERSHIP_LEDGER` files are optional, not prerequisites. Inspect their substance wherever it is recorded.

Audit the language and inheritance of historical state. Flag project instructions or memory that turn local evidence into permanent rules through labels such as `dead`, `retired`, `method-family exhausted`, `forbidden re-entry`, `saturation`, or `program stop`. Such labels are historical descriptions of exact evidence, never new user constraints. Current valid evidence and the live thesis override historical shorthand.

For consequential results, verify that state separately records observation, explanatory update, design consequence, and paper consequence. For negative method results, preserve what still worked, the positive residual, the component implicated, the next design inheritance, and evidence that would weaken the new interpretation. A ledger containing only verdicts is not an adequate research memory.

## Audit Value Continuity When It Matters

At a major method commitment, paper boundary, or possible portfolio pause, trace value from the original seed to the current concrete question. The current problem must earn significance at its own specificity: identify the naturally affected population or system, support and severity, accepted metric or decision changed, and why the implicated assumption matters across serious incumbents. A benchmark's prestige or a broad field's popularity cannot carry an arbitrarily narrow conditioned cell.

Distinguish the discovery slice, the value-bearing natural class, and the population claimed by the method or paper. A rare slice can isolate a cause, but the contribution needs either evidence that the cause matters more broadly or a primitive whose effect extends beyond the slice. If successive qualifications made the story cleaner while emptying its natural support, flag a problem-selection or abstraction issue rather than recommending another local experiment.

Read the original seed separately from the live paper framing, preferably from the user's original wording. Describe, in prose, the scope debt accumulated through every material qualifier, dataset/model/cell restriction, special protocol, and exception. Do not total it into a score. Ask what repays each important narrowing: standard-task value, a shared incumbent defect, natural prevalence or severity, cross-setting design leverage, or theoretical or operational consequence.

Require a visible paper-to-seed bridge. The current paper may be narrower or different from the seed's initial lens, but it should say what broad value remains, what the narrow evidence taught, and which completed or planned reintegration evidence returns the contribution to a natural task or system. A discovery slice cannot certify itself as value-bearing merely because its mechanism is clean.

Audit substrate choice explicitly. A dataset, operator, threshold band, architecture, or synthetic regime selected because the candidate method has headroom or clean identifiability is diagnostic evidence, not an independently earned contribution substrate.

## Audit A Major Compute Commitment

Before a costly decisive run, verify:

- a coherent causal and design thesis;
- a concrete value spine and enough natural support for the opportunity being pursued;
- the strongest honest contribution if every planned result succeeds, and why that prize is worth the intended venue and cost;
- a credible reintegration path from the discovery setting to an accepted task or natural system;
- a method that changes a load-bearing assumption;
- a strong simple alternative;
- a registered forecast and outcome interpretation;
- utility/general-capability checks;
- sanity-run, checkpoint, logging, and resume paths;
- realistic compute estimate and any user budget decision.

Block the expensive launch when the run is being used to answer a conceptual question that should be resolved by clearer design or a small discriminating test. Do not block because the method is risky; research methods are allowed to fail.

## Audit A Claim Or Paper

Verify:

- every number maps to a completed, read result;
- main comparisons are fair and current;
- scope matches models, datasets, seeds, and regimes actually tested;
- the supported scope remains consequential rather than merely precise;
- mechanism claims have isolating evidence;
- side effects and negative cases are represented;
- synthetic, sampled, or diagnostic evidence is labeled appropriately;
- no stronger weaker-story explanation remains untested;
- citations and related-work distinctions are accurate.

At a contribution-type change or paper-writing boundary, also audit the identity of the contribution. A method program does not become an analysis paper merely because its methods failed, and an analysis paper does not need a method if its own scientific case is strong. Determine whether the proposed object is natural and important, how it was selected, what evidence independently confirms it, what non-trivial explanation or measurement it contributes, and what scientific decision changes because of it.

Test the paper-to-seed bridge directly: remove the broad field name and benchmark prestige from the motivation, then ask whether the supported concrete object still has consequence; remove the private discovery qualifiers, then ask whether a shared principle or standard-surface result remains. This is not a demand that every paper cover the whole seed. It prevents a narrow paper from rhetorically borrowing a prize it did not earn.

Explicitly distinguish evidence stability from contribution maturity. A repeated relationship or well-mapped boundary can be stable while still lacking importance, actionability, causal or explanatory compression, novelty, or the comparison needed for a paper. Do not treat time spent, number of probes, or number of failed methods as maturity evidence.

Name the scope of the decision before auditing it: hypothesis, implementation, method branch, current paper, seed, or broader program. Read the original seed or program card as well as the latest state. Separate boundaries explicitly locked by the user from a contribution identity, method contract, or prohibition introduced by the agent during research. Agent-authored narrowing is revisable scientific state; it must not silently acquire the authority of the original program.

If the current paper identity has lost support, independently audit the strongest candidate identity suggested by the evidence instead of requiring the user to formulate it. This audit and reversible viability work are autonomous. User confirmation is needed before formal submission under a changed locked identity or venue, replacement of an explicitly locked deliverable, or a portfolio-level program pause. It is not needed merely to think through or test a candidate reframe.

For an analysis claim developed after extensive exploration, inspect selection provenance without treating exploration as misconduct. Ask whether thresholds, slices, variables, and explanations are supported only by the evidence that selected them; whether definitions or metric construction mechanically induce the headline relation; and whether an appropriate held-out contrast, robustness test, prediction, or intervention confirms the result. The right confirmation depends on the claim and need not be a fixed extra dataset.

Failed method lineage may motivate the analysis but cannot carry a bottleneck, impossibility, or mechanism claim unless the failures were valid isolating interventions. Use a deletion test: if removing the failed branches and their narrative leaves no independent object, confirmation, or consequence, classify the material as a valuable analysis asset rather than a paper contribution. `No paper contribution yet` is a valid boundary verdict.

Distinguish issues that invalidate the core claim from issues that narrow generality, revise mechanism wording, remove a component, or belong in limitations and future work. A paper may be `supported-with-limits` while the broader research program remains open. Do not require complete explanation of every anomaly as a precondition for a bounded correct claim.

`Supported-with-limits` still requires an important natural problem, the contribution type being formally claimed, fair strong comparisons, and sufficient evidence for that claim. Do not use scope language to promote a diagnostic slice, exploratory analysis asset, or redundant method into a venue-ready paper. Do not use a method-specific missing condition to reject a different contribution unless it also threatens that contribution's central claim.

Do not block paper writing because an arbitrary stage label is missing. Do require
the evidence-bearing `PAPER_ENTRY.md` check before complete manuscript work:
identity fingerprint, implemented positive object, fair decisive comparison, seed
provenance, contrary evidence, scope debt, and fresh independent
Prize/Fidelity/Entry review. The audit may assemble or challenge this packet; it may
not self-award `entry: PASS`.

## Audit Paper-Completion Opportunity

When deciding between more research and paper completion, ask:

- Is there an important natural core claim supported by completed evidence?
- Has the strongest semantically matched alternative been treated fairly?
- Is any unresolved issue likely to invalidate correctness, value, novelty, or the main comparison?
- Would the proposed next experiment change the method, claim, reviewer decision, or honest venue scope?
- Are the remaining questions better recorded as boundaries or later work?

If a mature positive object exists and no live claim-threatening issue remains, recommend paper completion instead of manufacturing another requirement. If a claim-threatening issue remains, name it precisely and identify the shortest evidence path that resolves it.

Do not complete a technically supported but negligible claim merely because it is easy to defend. Conversely, do not reopen a consequential bounded claim for experiments that only make it broader or more ornate. Correctness, significance, and contribution maturity must survive, while completeness is bounded by the current paper.

## Compare Projects Only When Asked

When the boundary is portfolio allocation across projects, compare compact current states rather than rerunning each project's full audit. Consider the natural problem already established, seed fidelity, accumulated scope debt, reintegration path, method primitive maturity, the next result most likely to change paper viability, distance to an honest paper, best-case scientific prize, and cost. Recommend where marginal tokens have the highest expected research and paper value. Do not use a mechanical score, equal allocation, or sunk cost as the decision rule.

## Audit A Portfolio-Level Pause

Verify:

- unfinished, interrupted, or unread work is not treated as negative evidence;
- the natural value failure itself has disappeared or the thesis is genuinely unsupported;
- one method loss is not being generalized to a method family or field;
- a certification, power, deployment, scale, or infrastructure limitation is not being mislabeled as failed actuation or thesis failure;
- easy and extreme regimes are not standing in for an untested realistic middle;
- the strongest higher-level explanation and design locus were considered;
- the conclusion states what is ruled out and what remains open;
- user confirmation will be obtained.

Before applying these checks to a seed or program, remove the current method name and method-specific promotion criteria and reread the original program scope. If only a branch or current paper is unsupported, name that narrower object and recommend the best research or paper-completion action rather than terminal language. A pause allocates resources; it does not establish that no future contribution exists.
