# Consequential Research Boundary Protocol

Use this reference only for a major compute, contribution-identity, manuscript,
submission, or portfolio decision. It is not a daily gate.

## Evidence packet

Collect durable paths to:

- original program and explicit user constraints;
- current claim and paper fingerprint;
- raw decisive results and strongest contrary evidence;
- exact evaluator, configs, seeds, provenance, and matched baselines;
- current method lineage and unresolved scope debt;
- the decision under review and its irreversible consequences.

## Independent review

Run a fresh `second-pi` review before the decision hardens into project state or
submission prose. Give the reviewer raw evidence before the lead author's preferred
interpretation. The reviewer should return:

- **Prize:** whether the strongest honest result matters at the supported scope;
- **Fidelity:** whether the current contribution still serves the original program;
- **Entry:** whether the evidence is mature enough for the proposed commitment;
- strongest alternative explanation and strongest attack;
- one recommended decision and evidence that would reverse it.

Reviewer output is advice, not user authority. Tool or reviewer unavailability does
not turn into a scientific verdict.

## Record the decision

Preserve observation, interpretation, design consequence, paper consequence,
chosen action, and reversal condition. Never rewrite a completed run's forecast or
success definition after seeing its result.

User confirmation remains necessary for destructive actions, exceptional spend,
formal submission, a changed user-locked venue or deliverable, or permanent closure
of the broad program.
