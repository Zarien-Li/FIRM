# Method Primitive Synthesis

Build a positive design object that makes the desired behavior natural by design.
Method formation is a sustained arc of implementation, contrast, failure, repair, and
simplification under one evolving principle.

## Enter With A Design Opportunity

Serious construction needs either an independently important known problem with a
consequential incumbent compromise or a reproduced natural failure that implicates a
changeable design assumption. Enter from the accepted surface, competent substrate, and
published rivals established by `research-foundation`, plus the design-giving account
from [signal-analysis.md](signal-analysis.md). An unresolved closest rival permits
exploration but keeps novelty provisional. A fashionable operator, formula, oracle,
probe, or positive private cell is not a design opportunity by itself.

A working account should connect the observed behavior to a change worth implementing
and identify what remains unknown. It need not already explain the full mechanism.
Construct and train when doing so is the best way to learn why the incumbent succeeds,
where it fails, or which computation should change. Let surprising outcomes revise the
account rather than requiring a complete causal story before implementation. A method
name remains provisional; it is not a substitute for that learning.

## Distinguish Sketch, Realization, And Paper Object

These are reasoning distinctions, not machine states or permission gates.

- A **candidate** is a formula, name, sketch, objective, oracle, smoke test, or
  pre-check.
- A **realization** is a trainable or executable implementation on the accepted task in
  which the intended computation activates and is compared under a meaningful matched
  treatment on a competent substrate.
- A **paper-bearing method** additionally establishes a claim-relevant advantage against
  the bounded set of decisive healthy published comparisons, with understood
  attribution, utility, tradeoffs, and scope.

The advantage can be primary-metric superiority or a meaningful Pareto improvement in
quality, robustness, coverage, latency, memory, compute, supervision, or adoption cost.
A method name, diagram, code path, pilot, or coherent story is not a maturity substitute.

## State The Primitive Before Accumulating Components

For the live candidate, write in ordinary scientific language:

- the observations and contrary cases it compresses;
- the default assumption being replaced;
- the one-sentence replacement principle;
- the load-bearing component or interaction and its unique prediction;
- the nearest rival, strongest simple composition, and non-redundant difference;
- attainable accepted-task value and the capability or cost to preserve;
- what the smallest real implementation teaches even if it loses;
- the low-friction adoption path and plausible transfer boundary.

Possible loci include representation or state, update/read rules, routing, memory,
objective factorization, credit assignment, training interaction, curriculum, data
treatment, and execution semantics. Surface form is not decisive: an objective,
threshold, verifier, policy, or wrapper can contribute when it embodies an irreducible
new rule with real value.

## Build For Principled Simplicity

Do not make a collection of hand-picked thresholds, score mixtures, dataset-specific
exceptions, routing rules, or fallback modules the proposed scientific contribution
merely because it improves a score. Such heuristics can be diagnostic probes, controls,
or temporary scaffolding; label them accordingly. An acronym, a learned gate, or an
optimization objective does not turn arbitrary composition into a principle.

Seek a compact governing idea that explains why the essential computation belongs
together, changes an incumbent assumption, and predicts behavior beyond the cases used
to invent it. Connect the objective, update, representation, or interaction to that
idea and to observed evidence. The account may develop empirically through construction;
do not require a theorem, differentiability, training, or a parameter-free algorithm as
proof of legitimacy. A justified approximation or threshold can be principled; a long
formula can still conceal a heuristic recipe.

Treat elegance as explanatory economy in the implemented method: essential components
share a reason to exist, improvements do not require a growing exception list, and
another researcher can adopt the core without reproducing project-specific tricks.
At meaningful design revisions, ask what can be removed, replaced, or unified while
preserving the intended behavior and claim-relevant advantage. Use existing attribution
evidence or a targeted simplification comparison where uncertainty matters, not a new
mandatory ablation campaign. A simpler variant that preserves the important benefit
should normally become the main method; retain extra machinery only for an evidenced
job or an honestly valued tradeoff.

Use the **80% deletion test** as a thought experiment, not a deletion quota. Remove
auxiliary scaffolding and ask what strong principle remains. Do not remove a component
that constructive evidence shows is necessary. If all the gain comes from patches and
the stated primitive contributes nothing, return to design rather than polishing its
story. If simplicity costs a material capability, report and reason about that tradeoff;
neither elegance nor a winning score alone establishes a paper-bearing contribution.

## Track Rival Gap And Method Debt

Keep the actual bill of materials beside the score:

- backbone and trainable parameters;
- external encoders, retrievers, rerankers, tools, teachers, routers, memory, stored
  targets, fallbacks, and supervision;
- training and inference calls, tokens, latency, memory, storage, and preprocessing;
- model-, dataset-, or evaluator-specific machinery.

For every addition, name the observed residual it addresses, the unique prediction it
creates, and whether deleting the original primitive removes the gain. Machinery that
rescues one cell, duplicates a rival, or carries the result without the primitive is
method debt. A larger system can be a legitimate systems contribution, but must be
named and compared as that deployment object rather than advertised as a simple
primitive.

Maintain the exact gap to the strongest functional rival on the intended value and cost
surface. Prefer replacing or simplifying debt over adding a separate patch for each
baseline.

## Improve Toward The Commissioned Target

Own the arc beyond the first working method. Connect implementation and real experiments
to deep result/model analysis, current primary literature, and healthy rivals' behavior;
use that understanding to implement the next repair, redesign, or simplification and
test it. These are coupled responsibilities, not a fixed sequence of calls or a demand
to repeat a literature search after every run. Read the baseline campaign's natural
successes and failures for inspiration on the development split, not just its leaderboard.
Choose collaborator work through the pipeline's collaboration reference when it adds
missing expertise; an unimplemented suggestion is not an improvement.

When the user asks for SOTA in at least 80% of cases, preserve that as an explicit
project objective rather than silently substituting a pilot win or a cost-only gain.
Define the comparison population from the agreed benchmark/backbone/protocol matrix
before examining outcomes. Ordinarily, one case is one meaningful cell with its
designated primary metric and operating condition, compared with the strongest healthy
eligible published method. Seeds, duplicate metrics, and beating multiple weak baselines
on the same cell do not multiply wins. Record full planned coverage and the win count;
missing or invalid cells remain unresolved obligations, not favorable exclusions, and
ties or statistically unresolved advantages are not clear wins. Preserve important
losses and costs rather than hiding them behind the aggregate fraction.

Use development results to optimize, then confirm the selected realization on the full
agreed evaluation without selecting versions on the test score. A local comparison win
supports SOTA language only when the comparable current published frontier is covered.
The 80% goal is neither a universal threshold for other projects nor an automatic
paper-readiness gate: meeting it does not replace attribution, meaningful utility,
simplicity, or honest limits. Conversely, do not rename a missed performance target as
a Pareto success without an explicit change to the commission.

Continue while the evidence supports a consequential next improvement within authorized
resources. When the present realization falls short, state the measured rival gap and
the evidence-directed next action, not a menu or another unmotivated version. Ask for
additional resources through the persistent PI when a ready, useful experiment genuinely
requires them. Do not promise that iteration guarantees the target; an unresolved goal
must remain visible rather than being claimed complete or pursued by repeated unchanged
attempts. Once the contribution is supported, carry it through writing and submission
readiness rather than stopping at the win fraction.

## Cultivate One Construction Arc

Keep the consequential problem and accumulated lessons connected across versions.
Explain what the previous realization taught, what changes now, what useful behavior
is retained, and what this attempt can reveal. The governing principle may be refined
or replaced when evidence warrants it; loyalty to the first formulation is not research
continuity. A genuinely different method does not inherit its predecessor's proven
maturity, but it can inherit code, valid evidence, and understanding. Do not let a
sequence of acronyms hide disconnected attempts.

Preserve a runnable, versioned comparison point while changing the method. After an
informative result, compare the intended computation with what actually changed and
what the strongest rival achieves. Choose the next alteration from that discrepancy.
When several mechanisms could explain it, use a targeted substitution or a joint
contrast that separates them; a single-variable change is useful, not compulsory.
Revisit the current comparison bundle before accumulating variants whose predecessors
remain uninterpreted. Construction may proceed without freezing an entire submission
matrix, and improving a positive version is legitimate when the expected value is clear.

If the principle is replaced, explain why the old account no longer guides useful work
and what evidence motivates the replacement. Keep the last competent implementation
as a reference when informative. A fresh method name must not reset the same unresolved
failure or lead to serial searches for a friendlier benchmark.

Across the arc, establish the real implementation, informative component substitutions
or interactions, paired utility at the point it matters, and eventually the decisive
published comparisons required by the claim. Early versions need not run the final
paper table. Their comparisons must be competent for the construction question they are
asked.

Use compact factorials or substitutions when components compensate or interact. Smoke
tests and activation checks establish plumbing; fresh matched evidence supports
paper-carrying necessity. Plan and execute these comparisons through `experiment-plan`
and `experiment-operations` rather than turning every version into a review ceremony.

## Repair From Evidence

When a realization misses its target, use the construction reading from
[signal-analysis.md](signal-analysis.md): preserve what ran, locate the earliest broken
link, and inherit the activated computation, useful residual, failed coupling, and next
discriminating prediction.

Judge an adjustment by its purpose and evidence, not its surface form. Rank, loss weight,
training duration, or schedule can test whether capacity, signal strength, or optimization
lets the intended computation develop. A new module can be equally uninformative when
it merely searches for a favorable score. Choose bounded comparisons that can resolve
the suspected cause, and keep development choices separate from confirmation.

A competent negative run establishes how that realization performed. Before extending
the conclusion to its principle, ask whether the implementation expressed it adequately
and whether the comparison tested its prediction. Repair verified defects and evaluate
the corrected realization under matched conditions. Repeated seeds are for stochastic
uncertainty, not a substitute for evidence-directed optimization or a search for a win.

One failed realization does not close a primitive, family, problem, or field. Continue
while evidence teaches a new component job or interaction and the natural problem
remains valuable; return to the broad program when another version would repeat an
answered prediction or elaborate a private cell. There is no fixed failure count.

## Use Co-Invention At A Real Invention Point

The lead PI owns construction. Use Gemini through
`research-pipeline/references/collaboration.md` when a real design opportunity,
informative failed coupling, or concrete rival gap makes a different principle useful.
Its proposal changes the method only after collision reading, implementation, and a
matched experiment. A concrete independent implementation, math, or novelty check can
help at any point through `research-review`; it does not replace co-invention or grant
permission to construct.

## Expand Only What The Principle Predicts

A contrastive task or regime may inform formation itself when it distinguishes a real
principle from a friendly-setting artifact; it need not wait for a fully mature method.
For broader claims, choose accepted tasks, model families, modalities, or regimes where
the working account predicts a meaningful contrast. Unexpected results may revise the
principle. Do not search surfaces for a favorable cell, and do not manufacture breadth
when no faithful contrast exists. Honest scope still has to serve the original problem.

## Durable Construction Note

Use the live project state for a compact arc. Create `METHOD_PRIMITIVE.md` only when
multi-actor coordination needs a durable account. Include the research basis, problem
model, principle, central design question, realization history, component jobs, rival
gap, utility contract, bill of materials, method debt, supported expansion, and next
discriminating action. The note records an evolving scientific object; it does not grant
paper maturity.
