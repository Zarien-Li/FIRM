# Method Primitive Synthesis

Build a positive design object that makes the desired behavior natural by design.
Method formation is a sustained arc of implementation, contrast, failure, repair, and
simplification under one evolving principle.

## Enter With A Design Opportunity

Serious construction needs either an independently important known problem with a
consequential incumbent compromise or a reproduced natural failure that implicates a
changeable design assumption. Before committing, know:

- the accepted task, value-bearing population, outcome, and competent substrate;
- a strong recent published incumbent, the nearest same-locus rivals, and the strongest
  ownership threat;
- the incumbent computation, useful default assumption, earliest success/failure
  divergence, intervention locus, and behavior to preserve;
- the discovery surface, intended reach, scope debt, contrary evidence, and route back
  to the accepted task.

The closest rival should be run or deeply inspected before novelty lock and dependent
optimization. An unresolved rival allows exploratory construction but keeps novelty and
paper maturity provisional. A fashionable operator, formula, oracle, probe, or positive
private cell is not a design opportunity by itself.

The problem account is sufficient when it explains why the incumbent normally succeeds,
why the same assumption produces the important failure, what load-bearing computation
must change, what must be preserved, and what behavioral contrast distinguishes the new
principle from the nearest rival. If those statements remain arbitrary, return to
`signal-analysis` rather than naming a method.

## Distinguish Sketch, Realization, And Paper Object

These are reasoning distinctions, not machine states or permission gates.

- A **candidate** is a formula, name, sketch, objective, oracle, smoke test, or
  pre-check.
- A **realization** is a trainable or executable implementation on the accepted task in
  which the intended computation activates and is compared under a meaningful matched
  treatment on a competent substrate.
- A **paper-bearing method** additionally establishes a claim-relevant advantage against
  the bounded set of decisive healthy published comparisons, with understood
  attribution, utility, tradeoffs, and scope.

The advantage can be primary-metric superiority or a meaningful Pareto improvement in
quality, robustness, coverage, latency, memory, compute, supervision, or adoption cost.
A method name, diagram, code path, pilot, or coherent story is not a maturity substitute.

## State The Primitive Before Accumulating Components

For the live candidate, write in ordinary scientific language:

- the observations and contrary cases it compresses;
- the default assumption being replaced;
- the one-sentence replacement principle;
- the load-bearing component or interaction and its unique prediction;
- the nearest rival, strongest simple composition, and non-redundant difference;
- attainable accepted-task value and the capability or cost to preserve;
- what the smallest real implementation teaches even if it loses;
- the low-friction adoption path and plausible transfer boundary.

Possible loci include representation or state, update/read rules, routing, memory,
objective factorization, credit assignment, training interaction, curriculum, data
treatment, and execution semantics. Surface form is not decisive: an objective,
threshold, verifier, policy, or wrapper can contribute when it embodies an irreducible
new rule with real value.

Use the **80% deletion test** throughout construction. Remove auxiliary modules,
dataset-specific vocabulary, scaffolding, and defensive additions until the remaining
principle is visible. Do not remove a component that constructive evidence shows is
causally necessary.

## Track Rival Gap And Method Debt

Keep the actual bill of materials beside the score:

- backbone and trainable parameters;
- external encoders, retrievers, rerankers, tools, teachers, routers, memory, stored
  targets, fallbacks, and supervision;
- training and inference calls, tokens, latency, memory, storage, and preprocessing;
- model-, dataset-, or evaluator-specific machinery.

For every addition, name the observed residual it addresses, the unique prediction it
creates, and whether deleting the original primitive removes the gain. Machinery that
rescues one cell, duplicates a rival, or carries the result without the primitive is
method debt. A larger system can be a legitimate systems contribution, but must be
named and compared as that deployment object rather than advertised as a simple
primitive.

Maintain the exact gap to the strongest functional rival on the intended value and cost
surface. Prefer replacing or simplifying debt over adding a separate patch for each
baseline.

## Cultivate One Construction Arc

Keep one central design question across versions. A successor realization must identify:

- the same replacement principle;
- the previous prediction that failed or remained unresolved;
- the evidence-implicated component or interaction being changed;
- the useful computation and paired utility being preserved;
- the new discriminating prediction.

Without that inheritance, the change is tuning, an unrelated candidate, or a new
episode whose maturity must be earned independently. Do not let a sequence of acronyms
hide a sequence of disconnected hypotheses.

Across the arc, establish the real implementation, informative component substitutions
or interactions, paired utility at the point it matters, and eventually the decisive
published comparisons required by the claim. Early versions need not run the final
paper table. Their comparisons must be competent for the construction question they are
asked.

Use compact factorials or substitutions when components compensate or interact. Smoke
tests and activation checks establish plumbing; fresh matched evidence supports
paper-carrying necessity. Plan and execute these comparisons through `experiment-plan`
and `experiment-operations` rather than turning every version into a review ceremony.

## Repair From Evidence

When a realization misses its target, preserve the verdict and locate the earliest
unsupported link: non-engagement, implementation or optimization failure, wrong proxy,
useful behavior coupled to regression, incorrectly fixed context, or redundancy with a
decisive alternative. Record the activated computation, useful residual, failed
coupling, and inherited prediction for the next version.

Classify the proposed change by what it does:

- **correction:** repairs a verified implementation, evaluator, optimizer, or
  integration defect;
- **construction:** changes a load-bearing component or interaction and creates a new
  prediction;
- **tuning:** changes only seed, coefficient, threshold, schedule, dose, or a nearby
  treatment.

A verified correction normally earns one clean matched rerun. A healthy, materially bad
paired development result calls for diagnosis or redesign, not a seed sweep. Repeat only
when nondeterminism or optimization variance makes the sign genuinely unresolved.

One failed realization does not close a primitive, family, problem, or field. Continue
while evidence teaches a new component job or interaction and the natural problem
remains valuable. Return to the broad program when another version would repeat an
answered prediction, elaborate a private cell, or preserve activity without plausible
accepted-task consequence. There is no fixed failure count.

## Use Co-Invention At A Real Invention Point

The lead PI owns the construction. Once empirical contact yields a real design
opportunity, Gemini may challenge the default assumption or propose a genuinely
different primitive. Give it the natural evidence, contrary cases, nearest rivals,
constraints, and unresolved design question; treat its response as a proposal, not a
result. Collision-read the idea, select at most one load-bearing change, implement the
smallest real realization, and let evidence reshape it.

Use Gemini again after an informative failure only when the failed coupling is known and
the next realization needs a different principle, or when a credible method still has a
named gap to a decisive rival. Do not invoke it merely because a score is negative or
record its proposal as method progress before implementation.

Independent Codex review belongs to `research-review`. It should examine an
artifact-grounded candidate or an exceptional paper-sized commitment, not invent or veto
the first method from an underdetermined observation.

## Expand Only What The Principle Predicts

After the realization survives its component and utility comparisons, consider another
accepted task, model family, modality, scale, natural system, reusable supervision or
training semantic, or a simpler realization only when the same principle predicts the
outcome. Choose an informative contrastive surface, not a favorable extra cell. When no
faithful contrast exists or the claim is intentionally narrow, state that scope instead
of manufacturing breadth.

## Durable Construction Note

Use the live project state for a compact arc. Create `METHOD_PRIMITIVE.md` only when
multi-actor coordination needs a durable account. Include the research basis, problem
model, principle, central design question, realization history, component jobs, rival
gap, utility contract, bill of materials, method debt, supported expansion, and next
discriminating action. The note records an evolving scientific object; it does not grant
paper maturity.
