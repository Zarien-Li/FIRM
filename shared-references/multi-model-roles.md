# Independent Model Collaboration

The lead researcher owns thesis, method, execution, synthesis, continuation, and paper
decisions. Other models contribute independent views; they are not pipeline stations,
authorities, or acceptance oracles.

For scientific collaboration, use the complete role definitions and maturity rules in
`research-review`. Gemini is the selective creative co-PI. Codex is a sparse
late-stage red-team verifier: normally one pass after a credible positive object before
paper-sized expansion and optionally one near-final factual or proof audit. Default to
zero Codex calls during discovery, interpretation, invention, cheap realization,
repair, and re-grounding.

Do not combine roles or review every result. Routine implementation, monitoring, and
decisions fixed by clear local evidence need no ceremonial multi-model call.

Use a fresh thread when independence matters. Use a reply only for the same unresolved
question after corrected facts or new evidence. Do not shop for agreement.

The lead PI records accepted, deferred, and rejected advice against local evidence.
Agreement is not empirical closure; disagreement is not an automatic experiment.

For independent artifact audits, follow `reviewer-independence.md`. Codex cannot veto
an exploratory principle, choose `PARK`, close a broad program, or change contribution
identity. Classify its objections as empirical falsification, verified ownership
collision, unverified risk, or reviewer preference; only the first two block the
affected claim or realization.

An unavailable model is an infrastructure issue. Preserve the packet, perform a
bounded self-adversarial pass, and continue non-dependent work. Do not guess model
names or convert failure into approval, rejection, or pause.
