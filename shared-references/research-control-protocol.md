# Research Control Protocol

Use this reference only for authority, evidence invalidation, contribution-type
changes, paper entry, destructive actions, and portfolio allocation. It is not the
daily research process.

## Authority

Only explicit user instructions, safety constraints, and live operational limits are
binding. Validated evidence controls claims. Agent interpretations, Codex advice,
registrations, drafts, tasks, memories, and summaries cannot create a user constraint.

Tag consequential state as `[USER]`, `[EVIDENCE]`, `[INTERPRETATION]`, `[REVIEW]`, or
`[HISTORY]`. A live `locked`, `forbidden`, `no re-entry`, or equivalent statement must
cite the exact user or safety source; otherwise it is interpretation or history.

Writing an interpretation into the authoritative state does not promote it into a
constraint. The state is authoritative about the project's current evidence and the
PI's current working synthesis; only the first is factual, and the second remains
revisable by later evidence or better reasoning. A self-authored gate, threshold,
forecast, route choice, paper hold, or experiment registration governs the exact run
or claim for which it was created. It cannot prohibit future method formation, repair,
or paper harvest unless an explicit user or safety boundary says so.

The AI researcher autonomously chooses reversible scientific actions inside explicit
scope and authorized resources. User confirmation is reserved for destructive or
irreversible operations, exceptional spending, credentials or safety, formal
submission/public release, and changes to explicitly locked project, venue,
deliverable, or portfolio scope. A short `continue` authorizes the described action,
not every causal story or restriction surrounding it.

When a temporary decision has served its evidentiary purpose, revise or remove it from
the live synthesis. Preserve its provenance with the run rather than accumulating it
as a permanent restriction. Facts survive replacement-style state updates; obsolete
strategies do not.

## Evidence Invalidation

When a material validity failure is verified, remove the affected evidence from active
claims and propagate invalidation through dependent caches, runs, analyses, tables,
figures, drafts, summaries, and handoffs using `evidence-lineage.md`. Preserve original
artifacts and the reason for invalidation.

A later narrative, review, timestamp, or limitation cannot revive invalid evidence.
Repair, rerun, or redesign. Invalidation blocks only dependent claims and commitments;
it does not close the broader program.

## Program And Paper Identity

Keep the original program separate from a current paper candidate. A paper identity is
defined by its problem, contribution type, positive object, primitive, claimed
population or task, primary value metric, and decisive comparison.

When this identity materially changes, preserve the old draft as history and return
the new candidate to provisional status. Reassess program fidelity, scope movement,
nearest-rival ownership, reintegration, and evidence needs. Evidence and lessons may
transfer; maturity does not.

Identity reconciliation happens at meaningful boundaries, not after every experiment.

## Contribution-Type Firewall

A method project cannot become analysis, measurement, mechanism, theory, or systems
work merely because interventions failed. A different contribution type requires:

- an independently valuable natural object that survives deleting failed-method
  history;
- confirmation independent of the evidence that selected it;
- a scientific or operational consequence;
- comparison with the strongest existing account;
- a fresh paper identity grounded in the new object's evidence.

The lead PI may make this reversible scientific decision unless it changes an
explicitly locked project or deliverable. Failed interventions may motivate the
question but cannot supply importance, confirmation, or impossibility by their count.

## Paper Entry

Before paper entry, use notes or a clearly marked research draft to organize a credible
positive object. Early writing may expose gaps; it does not confer readiness.

Submission-oriented expansion requires a lead-PI synthesis based on raw evidence. The
compact `PAPER_ENTRY.md` should establish:

- important natural or accepted problem and program bridge;
- exact contribution identity and positive object;
- decisive fair incumbent and nearest-rival comparison;
- reliability appropriate to the claim;
- utility, capability, latency, and cost tradeoffs that matter;
- provenance, strongest contrary evidence, scope debt, and boundaries that materially
  change interpretation.

An optional near-final independent audit may verify the fixed object under the sparse
Codex policy; it is not a prerequisite for writing and cannot create or revoke the
contribution identity by preference alone. Return entry to hold if a decisive rival wins, population or primary metric changes,
seed provenance is selective, a fair control reverses the claim, the advertised object
was not implemented, only a private cell remains, contribution type changes, or
central evidence is invalidated. Reconcile the science; do not build a defensive grid
to preserve a title.

## Completion And Portfolio Allocation

Once unresolved issues can no longer change correctness, importance, novelty, the
positive object, scope, or a likely reviewer decision, finish the bounded paper.
Research may remain open afterward.

Pause or reallocate a program by comparing marginal scientific and paper value across
the portfolio: natural importance, fidelity to the original program, positive-object
maturity, next evidence, compute cost, scope debt, and plausible upside. A pause is a
resource decision, not proof that a field is exhausted. Archival or deletion requires
user confirmation.

## Compaction Safety

Keep one concise authoritative state with raw-artifact links. Compacted summaries,
memory, reviews, and historical ledgers are locators, not evidence or permission.
