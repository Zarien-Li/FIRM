# Effort Guidance

Effort controls how deeply a task is pursued, not how many artifacts, ideas, papers, datasets, seeds, or review rounds must be produced.

## Stable Integrity

Changing effort never weakens:

- provenance and experiment integrity;
- fair, semantically matched baselines;
- citation and claim accuracy;
- reviewer independence when an independent audit is requested;
- sanity checks appropriate to the blast radius;
- user control over exceptional spending and irreversible actions.

## Interpret Effort Qualitatively

- **lite:** answer the live question with the smallest credible evidence and avoid breadth that will not change the decision.
- **balanced:** pursue enough depth to make a sound research or product decision, then stop when additional work has low expected value.
- **max:** investigate important alternatives, contrary evidence, and transfer or robustness questions that could materially change the claim.
- **beast:** use unusually broad or deep effort for a genuinely high-value objective, while still consolidating once the decisive uncertainty is resolved.

These are intent descriptions, not multipliers or quotas. A lite integrity check can still be strict. A beast research task may need one decisive experiment rather than twenty cheap probes.

## Allocate Effort By Uncertainty

Spend more when:

- evidence is contradictory or high stakes;
- the strongest alternative is unclear;
- a method choice commits substantial compute;
- a claim's value or generality depends on an untested realistic regime;
- a paper is near submission and an error would be costly.

Spend less when:

- the answer is already fixed by reliable evidence;
- another item would repeat the same information;
- the work expands a diagnostic atlas without changing the method or claim;
- the requested breadth would manufacture ceremony rather than insight.

Do not use effort labels to automate skill chains, force session changes, or delay a scientifically justified method run.
