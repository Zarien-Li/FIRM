# Case 2: The Paper Drifts From The Seed

## Situation

The project begins in a high-value field such as knowledge editing. After many
diagnostics, the cleanest effect exists only in a model-specific, frequency-
conditioned slice. The agent starts treating that slice as the paper.

## Common Agent Failure

The local result is real, so the agent keeps drilling inward. It stops asking
whether solving the private cell changes a standard task, a mainstream system,
or a decision that the community values.

The result can become statistically clean while the paper becomes irrelevant.

## ResearcherOS Response

The researcher keeps three objects visible:

- original program: the important field and user value;
- current paper candidate: the bounded claim supported now;
- scope debt: what must be reconnected before the candidate can represent the
  program.

Codex is called early for Prize/Fidelity:

- If every best-case result in the slice came true, would the paper matter?
- Is the slice a microscope for a shared system defect or the final population?
- Which standard task or natural workflow would change if the mechanism were
  fixed?

## Starting Prompt

```text
Use research-review for an early Prize/Fidelity review. Compare the original
program with the current candidate claim and evidence population. Identify
scope debt, perform the best-case prize test, and design the shortest
reintegration path to a standard task or natural workflow. Do not discard a
real local result, but do not let a private slice silently become the seed.
```

## Expected Artifacts

- `PIPELINE_STATE.md` names original program, candidate paper, and scope debt.
- `PRIZE_FIDELITY_REVIEW.md` records the second-PI attack.
- `REINTEGRATION_PLAN.md` connects the diagnostic to standard value.
- Paper writing remains blocked until the positive object survives outside the
  private slice.

## Why It Matters

Research agents are good at discovering progressively narrower truths. They
need an explicit mechanism for preserving why the research was worth doing.
